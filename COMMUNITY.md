# Community & Support

Read this before opening an issue, a discussion, or reaching out.

## What this project is

This is solo work, built independently with the assistance of AI tools. There is no
team, no company, and no obligation to anyone using or looking at this code.

## What to expect

- **No support obligation.** This is released so people can see real, working code
  and use it if they want to - not as a supported product. If you can't get it
  building or running on your own from [`HOWTO.md`](HOWTO.md), that's a real
  limitation worth being honest about, not something owed to you as a fix.
- **No obligation to explain decisions.** Design choices, priorities, and what gets
  worked on next are made at the maintainer's own discretion. Disagreement is fine;
  an expectation of debate or justification is not.
- **This is an initial release.** Whether, when, and how much more gets released
  after this is entirely the maintainer's own call, made on their own timeline. No
  promises either way.

## What's welcome

- **Forking.** Take this in your own direction, build on it, use it as a reference -
  genuinely welcome, within the terms of [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md).
- **Real collaboration**, for anyone who wants to build alongside this project rather
  than fork away from it - join the Discord: **https://discord.gg/bhst2CNWy**. That's
  the actual channel for questions, discussion, and coordination - not GitHub issues
  or discussions.

## Conduct

Be respectful and professional. Disrespectful, entitled, or hostile behavior toward
the maintainer or other contributors won't be engaged with. This applies on Discord
as much as anywhere else.
